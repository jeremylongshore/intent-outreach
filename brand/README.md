## Relationship to Intent Solutions

Intent Outreach has its own practical voice for property research, business contacts and outreach preparation. Intent Solutions is the company that builds and operates the work. The product inherits the company's commitment to specific outcomes, inspectable evidence and clear operating responsibility. It does not inherit a generic company homepage pitch as its product explanation.

The established company positioning is accountable AI implementation. Its public expression is [Intent Solutions](https://intentsolutions.io/). The project translates that principle into a concrete question: what information does an operator need to decide the next step for this property, business or contact?

The user requested a locally owned brand and a broader real estate showcase. The wording in these files is the team's implementation of that direction; it is not a claim that the user personally approved every phrase.

## Claim precedence

Current user direction governs the work. Verified implementation and observed results govern capability claims. These brand files govern how those facts are presented. A plan, credential, connector file or provider's marketing page does not establish a successfully operated workflow.

## Maintaining this brand

Update the relevant project file when the offer, audience or verified capability changes. Keep examples distinguishable from observed results. Keep technical proof close enough to inspect while leading public pages with the visitor's work and desired result.

Do not add testimonials, conversion figures, customer results, market coverage or readiness claims without a source that supports that exact statement. Do not store credentials, personal contact records or customer research in this directory.
