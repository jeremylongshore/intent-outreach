# Workflow inquiry conversion copy

The conversion is an explicit project inquiry with a useful brief and reply details.
The interactive example remains available without a signup. Local brand authority:
[voice](../../brand/voice.md), [positioning](../../brand/positioning.md).

## Implemented form section

**Bring the workflow you wish you had.**

Tell us what you want to find, organize or act on. Send the brief you shaped here,
and our team can review the sources, tools and next step with you.

Start with one useful output: a shortlist, a follow-up desk, a client pack or
something your business needs.

Sending starts a project conversation. It does not place an order or subscribe
you to marketing emails.

Fields: editable workflow brief, name, reply email, optional company.

Primary action: **Send my workflow brief**.

Success: Your workflow brief has been received. Our team will review it and use
the email you provided to respond about your project. Your copy remains here.

The shared private Buzz `leads` channel is the team's existing intake destination.
The source URL and brief give the reviewer enough context to identify the job,
assess sources and access, and propose the next scoping conversation. No response
SLA, price, automated follow-up or guaranteed integration is promised.

## Copy review and test candidates

The previous CTA required copying a brief, leaving the page, and pasting it into
another form. The implemented version submits the reviewed brief directly and
explains the response path. This removes a concrete handoff step; it is not evidence
of a measured conversion lift. There are no testimonials or urgency claims to invent.

Alternative headings for later comparison:

- Shape a workflow around your business.
- Turn your criteria into a useful next step.
- What should your team have ready each morning?
- Start with the output your business needs.
- Bring your process. Build your next step.

Alternative body: Describe the work you repeat and the result you want in hand.
We can review your criteria, existing tools and data needs, then discuss a bounded
first build.

Potential tests, after enough real traffic exists:

| Comparison | Hypothesis | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current heading vs “Shape a workflow around your business” | Direct customization language may make the service clearer. | Accepted inquiries per eligible visit. |
| “Send my workflow brief” vs “Ask us to scope this workflow” | A conversation-oriented action may better match visitor intent. | Accepted inquiries and fit after team review. |
| Current brief-first form vs contact fields first | Ordering may affect how readily visitors review and complete the form. | Completion and validation failure rates. |

These are unrun hypotheses, not projected lift. Avoid sending names, emails or
brief content into analytics. There is no new analytics or experiment system in
this change; Buzz remains the operational intake destination.
